2. What Information We Collect & Why

When you submit our enquiry or recipe newsletter forms, we collect only the information needed to respond to your request and safely adapt sessions for the learner:

  • Contact details: Your name, email address, and optional telephone/WhatsApp number.
  • Service preferences: Which programme, weekly slot, or school workshop you are enquiring about.
  • Learner sensory & dietary notes (optional): Information you choose to share about sensory preferences, allergies, or EHCP goals so our teachers can prepare a safe, calm session.

Lawful basis (UK GDPR): Your explicit consent when submitting the enquiry form (Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) for any special category health/SEN information you voluntarily provide) and steps prior to entering into a service agreement (Article 6(1)(b)).

3. Who Sees Your Information

Your enquiry is strictly confidential. It is only accessed by our founding educators (Anna Maria Racco and Lucas Rambourg) to prepare and deliver your consultation or cooking session. We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketers.
