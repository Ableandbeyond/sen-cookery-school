Heart Lattice Pie ❤️

A beautiful rustic pie with bright red cherry filling.

🛒 What You Need

🧑‍🍳 Step-by-Step

🧼

Step 1: Wash Hands

Wash your hands carefully before touching the pastry.

🥧

Step 2: Prepare the Base

Unroll one pastry sheet and gently press it into your round pie dish.

🍒

Step 3: Pour the Filling

Spoon your bright red cherry or strawberry filling into the pastry base and spread it flat.

🔪

Step 4: Cut the Strips

Unroll the second pastry sheet. Use a child-safe tool to cut long, straight strips.

🕸️

Step 5: Make the Lattice

Lay the strips across the top of the pie in a criss-cross pattern. Over, under, over, under!

❤️

Step 6: Heart Cutouts

Use extra pastry to cut out little hearts and place them on top of the pie.

🖌️

Step 7: Brush & Bake

Brush the pastry gently with egg wash. Ask an adult to bake at 200°C for 25-30 minutes.
