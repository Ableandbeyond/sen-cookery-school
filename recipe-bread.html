Anna's Easy Bread 🍞

Get your hands messy and feel the dough!

🛒 What You Need

🧑‍🍳 Step-by-Step

🧼

Step 1: Wash Hands

Wash your hands and clear a large space on the table.

🥣

Step 2: Mix Dry Ingredients

Put the flour, salt, and yeast into a big bowl. Stir them together.

💧

Step 3: Add Water & Oil

Pour in the warm water and olive oil. Mix it until it forms a sticky dough.

🤲

Step 4: Knead the Dough

Take the dough out and push, pull, and stretch it on the table for 10 minutes. It should feel smooth and stretchy!

⏳

Step 5: Let it Rise

Put the dough back in the bowl, cover it with a towel, and wait 1 hour for it to grow twice as big!

🥖

Step 6: Shape It

Gently punch the dough and shape it into a round loaf. Put it on a baking tray.

🔥

Step 7: Bake

Ask an adult to bake it at 220°C for 30 minutes until it sounds hollow when tapped.
