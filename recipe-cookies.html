Sensory Star Cookies

Fun, colourful, and gentle to make—ideal for practising mixing, rolling, and shape cutting.

What You Need (Ingredients & Tools)

Step-by-Step Visual Guide

Step 1: Wash & Dry Hands

Wash your hands with soap and warm water for 20 seconds, then dry them on a clean towel.

Step 2: Mix Butter & Sugar

Put the soft butter and sugar into a large bowl. Stir with a wooden spoon or silicone spatula until smooth and creamy.

Step 3: Crack & Stir the Egg

Crack the egg into a small cup first, then pour it into your mixing bowl and stir gently.

Step 4: Add Flour & Bring Dough Together

Tip in the flour. Mix with a spoon first, or use your hands if you enjoy the soft dough texture, until it forms a ball.

Step 5: Roll & Cut the Stars

Roll the dough flat on a lightly floured board and press down firmly with your star cutter.

Step 6: Decorate Your Stars

Place the stars onto a lined baking tray and gently press colourful sweets or sprinkles on top.

Step 7: Bake with an Adult

Ask an adult to bake the tray at 180°C (160°C fan) for 10–12 minutes until lightly golden, then cool before eating.
