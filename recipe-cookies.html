Sensory Star Cookies ✨

Fun, colourful, and easy to make! Let's get mixing.

🛒 What You Need

🧑‍🍳 Step-by-Step

🧼

Step 1: Wash Hands

Wash your hands with soap and warm water for 20 seconds. Dry them on a clean towel.

🥣

Step 2: Mix Butter & Sugar

Put the soft butter and sugar into a big bowl. Mix them together until they are soft and fluffy.

🥚

Step 3: Crack the Egg

Crack the egg gently and add it to your bowl. Mix it all together!

🌾

Step 4: Add Flour

Pour in the flour. Use your hands to squeeze and knead it into a big dough ball. Feel the texture!

⭐

Step 5: Cut the Stars

Roll out your dough nice and flat. Use your star cutter to press out the shapes.

🍭

Step 6: Decorate

Press the colourful candies onto your stars. Make them look beautiful!

⏲️

Step 7: Bake

Ask an adult to put the tray in the oven for 10-12 minutes at 180°C. Wait for them to turn golden!
