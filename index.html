Meet Cookery Chef Anna Maria Racco

The culinary heart behind Able and Beyond, sharing her passion for inclusive cooking and storytelling.

A Life of Culinary Education & Movement

🎓 25+ Yrs SEN & PMLD Specialist 💃 Dance Movement Therapist 👨‍🍳 College Culinary Educator 📚 Children's Book Author

I grew up in Italy in a home where cooking was part of everyday life and joy. Experimenting beside my grandmother, I discovered flavours, textures, and the tactile pleasure of creating with my hands—a traditional foundation that still guides my teaching today.

Moving to London in 1986, I trained as a Dance Movement Therapist and spent over 25 years working with SEN children and adults across schools, colleges, nursing homes, and hospitals. I combine movement, sensory regulation, and kitchen creativity to help students build self-expression and motor confidence.

Specialising in inclusive education, I teach culinary arts and life skills to mainstream and SEN learners, including those with PMLD (Profound and Multiple Learning Disabilities). Today, I also teach at Capital City College Group in London, dedicated to passing on practical independence through adaptive, joyful cooking.

The Eagle and the Parakeets

Beyond the kitchen, Anna is the proud author of the children's book The Eagle and the Parakeets, weaving themes of inclusivity, bravery, and friendship that reflect the exact values she brings to every cooking session.

🧩 Inclusive & Neuro-Affirming

Our SEN & Sensory Approach

Every learner experiences textures, sounds, and smells differently. Our sessions are built from the ground up to remove anxiety and celebrate small steps toward independence.

📋

Visual Step-by-Step Symbols

Every recipe is broken down into clear, symbol-supported steps so learners know what is happening now and what comes next—reducing cognitive overload.

🖐️

Zero-Pressure Texture Exploration

Whether a learner prefers touching dough directly, using a silicone spatula, or smelling herbs first, we honour their sensory boundaries at all times.

🔪

Adaptive & Safe Equipment

We guide families and schools on colour-coded non-slip boards, nylon safety knives, and easy-grip tools that build fine motor confidence safely.

💃

Movement & Emotional Regulation

Drawing on Cookery Chef Anna’s 25+ years in SEN and Dance Movement Therapy, sessions maintain a calm, rhythmic pace tailored to each student's energy.
